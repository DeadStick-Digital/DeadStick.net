Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. This policy covers DeadStick Utilities for Mac, published by DeadStick Digital LLC.

Privacy summary Your files and scan results stay on your Mac

File contents, file names and scan results stay on your Mac. DeadStick Utilities has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no company-operated cloud service.

What Stays On Your Mac

DeadStick Utilities inspects only the folders you choose. File contents, file names, file paths, sizes, duplicate comparisons, scan results and Recovery records stay on your Mac. The app does not send them to DeadStick.

Files you move to Recovery are held on their original volume until you restore, export or permanently delete them.

What We Do Not Have

  • No DeadStick account.
  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No company-operated cloud service.

DeadStick Utilities has no background agent. It runs when you open it.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds. DeadStick does not receive your payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Support Export

A support export is created only when you choose to create one, and you save it locally yourself. Nothing is sent automatically. Review it before sharing it, and do not include personal files, passwords or payment details in a support message.

Website And Support Email

Visits to this website and support messages you send voluntarily are covered by the DeadStick Digital privacy policy.

Children

DeadStick Utilities is a general utility app and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy and support requests can be sent through the DeadStick Digital message form. Do not include private files or personal data in the first message.
