How It Works

Engineered for media servers.

1. Smart Bitrate-Aware Preloading

Instead of using a one-size-fits-all cache size that under-buffers high-bitrate 4K Remuxes and wastes SSD space on smaller episodes, HeadStart automatically sizes each preload to match the video's true bitrate alongside its subtitles.

2. Seamless Drive Handoff

Most library browsing and preloaded file metadata checks are served directly from NVMe without waking sleeping drives. Only when someone actually presses Play does HeadStart wake that single target drive in the background and smoothly hand off the stream once it is up to speed.

3. Automatic Free-Space Watchdog

Using your NVMe drive for downloads or daily cache staging? HeadStart monitors your free space continuously. If a large download batch needs room, HeadStart automatically frees up low-priority preload data so your downloads never slow down, then rebuilds it when space recovers.

4. Polite Background Harvesting

HeadStart prioritizes your newest movies and active TV shows first and harvests across a few drives at a time so the rest of your array stays spun down, quiet, and cool.

5. Universal Client Support

Every media app connected to your server — Plex, Infuse, Jellyfin, Emby, VLC, and IINA — gets instant cold-drive playback automatically with zero client configuration.

6. Non-Destructive & 1-Click Removable

HeadStart is strictly read-only and never modifies, moves, or renames a single file on your media array. Stopping or removing the plugin immediately returns your server to its standard setup without restarting Plex.
