Private VPN

CarrierPigeonVPN

Your own iPhone as a private VPN for your Mac: an encrypted tunnel between two devices you own, with no account and no VPN company in the middle.

Contact support → Learn more

Overview

Pair your iPhone and your Mac once with a QR code. When you connect from the Mac, its traffic, including DNS lookups, travels inside an encrypted tunnel to your iPhone and leaves through your iPhone's cellular connection. Disconnect on the Mac and the iPhone stops right away.

Privacy

CarrierPigeonVPN never sees the contents, destinations, or metadata of your traffic. Pairing keys stay on your devices in the system keychain, and subscription status is checked through Apple and RevenueCat. As required by Apple's App Store Review Guideline 5.4, DeadStick Digital LLC does not sell, use, or disclose to third parties any data collected by CarrierPigeonVPN for any purpose. Before you use it, the app shows exactly what data is involved. See the Privacy Policy.

Pricing and subscription

An auto-renewing annual subscription, bought in the iPhone app: a 7-day free trial, then $9.99 per year (US price; local prices vary by country). The Mac app is free.

  • Starting the free trial costs nothing. When the 7 days end, $9.99 is charged to your Apple Account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • After that, the subscription renews automatically each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings at any time. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If you cancel, the VPN keeps working until the end of the trial or paid period you already have.

See the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

Requirements

iPhone: iOS 26 or later, with a cellular plan that includes Personal Hotspot.
Mac: macOS 26 or later.
