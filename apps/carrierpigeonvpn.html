CarrierPigeonVPN

A private VPN for iPhone, Android phones, iPad, Mac, and Windows: an encrypted tunnel between two devices you own, with no account and no VPN company in the middle.

Download → Contact support

Overview

Privacy that goes where you go. CarrierPigeonVPN is being built for iPhone, Android phones, iPad, Mac, and Windows. Pair your own devices into a private connection, so your browsing stays between your devices. One click to connect, one click to stop.

Privacy

Your traffic stays yours. It travels only between the devices you pair, never through a DeadStick server, so we never see what you send, where it goes, or when. Pairing keys stay on your devices in secure system storage. The only outside check is your subscription status; Apple subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat, which works on our behalf. DeadStick Digital LLC does not sell, use, or disclose to third parties any data for any purpose. Before you use it, the app shows exactly what data is involved. See the Privacy Policy.

Pricing and subscription

The terms below apply to Apple subscriptions. The Mac and Windows companion apps are free and use the connection your phone shares. Android purchase details will be confirmed before release.

An auto-renewing annual subscription, bought in the iPhone app: a 7-day free trial, then $9.99 per year (US price; local prices vary by country).

  • Starting the free trial costs nothing. When the 7 days end, $9.99 is charged to your Apple Account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • After that, the subscription renews automatically each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings at any time. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If you cancel, the VPN keeps working until the end of the trial or paid period you already have.

See the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

Requirements

iPhone: iOS 26 or later, with a cellular plan that includes Personal Hotspot.
Android phones and iPad: Planned support; device and operating-system requirements will be confirmed before release.
Mac: macOS 26 or later.
Windows: Windows 11 on an x64 or Arm64 PC, with an administrator account to install, Wi-Fi, and an iPhone running CarrierPigeonVPN to share its connection.
