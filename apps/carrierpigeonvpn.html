Paired-device networking

CarrierPigeonVPN

A private WireGuard tunnel between two devices you already own. CarrierPigeonVPN lets your Mac use your paired iPhone's cellular connection through one encrypted tunnel, with no account and no DeadStick servers in the path.

Contact support → Learn more

Overview

CarrierPigeonVPN has two parts: an iPhone app that shares its cellular connection, and a Mac app that uses it. The Mac joins your iPhone's Personal Hotspot, and all of the Mac's traffic then travels inside an encrypted WireGuard tunnel to your iPhone, which sends it out over your own cellular plan.

You pair the two once by scanning a QR code; the apps exchange keys over Bluetooth. After that you connect from the Mac. While connected, the Mac's DNS lookups also go through the tunnel. Your iPhone can remember up to four paired devices and shares with one Mac at a time.

How it works

  • On iPhone, the tunnel runs in a Packet Tunnel extension (NEPacketTunnelProvider). Its only job is to carry your Mac's traffic to your iPhone and out through the iPhone's cellular connection.
  • On the Mac, CarrierPigeonVPN's own network extension routes the Mac's traffic, including DNS, into the tunnel while you are connected.
  • The exit point is your iPhone — your SIM, your plan, your account.
  • When you disconnect on the Mac, the iPhone is told and stops sharing. If the two lose touch, the iPhone stops sharing on its own after about a minute.

What CarrierPigeonVPN is not

CarrierPigeonVPN is not a commercial VPN service. We do not operate exit nodes, do not terminate your traffic on DeadStick-owned servers, and do not anonymize you on the public internet. Your exit point is your own phone on your own cellular plan — which is why it is your responsibility to use CarrierPigeonVPN in a manner that complies with your carrier's terms of service and acceptable-use policy. DeadStick Digital makes no representation that any particular use of CarrierPigeonVPN is permitted under any particular plan.

VPN scope and disclosures

CarrierPigeonVPN uses Apple's Network Extension VPN APIs on iPhone and Mac for a single purpose: to route your Mac's traffic through your own paired iPhone. We do not inspect, log, collect, share, or transmit the contents, destinations, or metadata of any traffic that flows through the tunnel. The tunnel carries only your own traffic between two devices you control, and the app does not modify traffic from other apps on the iPhone.

As required by Apple's App Store Review Guideline 5.4, DeadStick Digital LLC does not sell, use, or disclose to third parties any data collected by CarrierPigeonVPN for any purpose. Before you use the tunnel, the app shows a privacy screen that explains exactly what data is involved.

Pricing and subscription

CarrierPigeonVPN is an auto-renewing annual subscription, bought in the iPhone app: a 7-day free trial, then $9.99 per year (US price; local prices vary by country). One subscription covers sharing to your paired Mac; the Mac app itself is free.

  • Starting the free trial costs nothing. When the 7 days end, $9.99 is charged to your Apple Account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • After that, the subscription renews automatically each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings at any time. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If you cancel, sharing keeps working until the end of the trial or paid period you already have. After that, the iPhone stops sharing until you subscribe again.

See the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

How your data is handled

CarrierPigeonVPN never sees the contents, destinations, or metadata of your traffic — that traffic flows directly between your two devices and then out of your iPhone's own network connection. Local configuration (WireGuard keys, paired-device identifiers) is stored on your devices in the system keychain. Subscription status is checked through Apple and RevenueCat. See the privacy policy for the complete treatment.

Requirements

iPhone: iOS 26 or later, with a cellular plan that includes Personal Hotspot.
Mac: macOS 26 or later.
Windows, Apple TV, and Android versions are planned and not yet available.
