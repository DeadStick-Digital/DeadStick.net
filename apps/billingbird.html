BillingBird · Professional invoicing

The invoicing app that never owns your books.

Create professional invoices, scan receipts, track payments, and keep your business organized while your records stay on your device. Sync is optional and uses your own cloud account. DeadStick Digital has no bookkeeping server storing your data.

Your business data belongs to your business.

Most invoicing apps synchronize through company-owned servers. BillingBird keeps your records on your device. If you choose to sync, the app uses your own supported iCloud or Google Drive account instead of a DeadStick Digital bookkeeping server.

Data ownership

Your invoices, receipts, and customer records stay on your device. If you enable sync, your information is stored in your own supported cloud account. DeadStick Digital has no server that stores or reads your bookkeeping. See the privacy policy for details.

Built around ownership

  • Local-first records that stay available on your device.
  • No BillingBird account required.
  • Optional sync through your own supported cloud account.
  • Export everything at any time.
  • Works offline.

Built for business

  • Create and send professional PDF invoices with line items, categories, and tax rules.
  • Scan receipts and attach them to a client or expense report.
  • Track payments, partial payments, and outstanding balances.
  • Keep a client directory with notes, default rates, and contact history.
  • Build reports from your invoices, payments, and expenses.
  • Choose from six themes with full light and dark modes.

Your data isn't rented.

Export a full .billingbird archive whenever you choose. Your business records are portable, with no lock-in and no dependence on BillingBird servers.

Why we built BillingBird this way

Small businesses shouldn't have to upload years of invoices, receipts, client lists, and payment history to someone else's servers just to send an invoice. BillingBird was designed so syncing your devices doesn't require giving us your books. You stay in control. Always.

Accessibility

BillingBird supports VoiceOver on iOS and TalkBack on Android, respects Dynamic Type, and ships with a high-contrast palette for users with low vision. If you find an accessibility issue, please send us a note through the contact form.

Technical specifications

Platforms: iOS 17 or later on iPhone and iPad; Android 12 or later.
Implementation: React Native 0.76 with Expo SDK 52 and React Native's New Architecture.
