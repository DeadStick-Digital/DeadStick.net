Why BillingBird is different

BillingBird pairs the finish a small business needs with a privacy model that keeps its books under its control. The same focused workflow reaches the Apple devices you already use, without requiring a BillingBird account or a DeadStick-hosted bookkeeping service.

Private by architecture

Invoices, receipts, clients, payments, and reports are stored on your device. Optional private iCloud continuity uses your Apple account, not a readable DeadStick database.

Professional where it matters

Build polished customer documents, track the work behind them, and export durable records without giving up clarity, accessibility, or presentation quality.

Built for iPhone, iPad & Mac

Capture work where it happens, review it on a larger screen, and move your active books between Apple devices through an optional, guarded iCloud handoff.

Your records have a clear path

  1. Local device storage. Your active books live in BillingBird's private app storage and remain available offline.
  2. Optional private iCloud backup and handoff. When enabled, BillingBird uses your private iCloud account to back up and hand off the active data set between your Apple devices.
  3. No readable DeadStick bookkeeping database. DeadStick Digital does not receive a server-side copy of your invoices, receipts, clients, or reports.

The iCloud workflow is a guarded, single-active-device handoff—not real-time collaboration or simultaneous editing. Apple provides iCloud and App Store services, and RevenueCat may provide subscription entitlement services; those services are separate from your bookkeeping records.

Professional work, from estimate to archive

  • Create polished estimates and invoices with line items, categories, and tax rules.
  • Record manual, partial, and completed payments and keep outstanding balances visible.
  • Build reports from invoices, payments, expenses, and organized client records.
  • Prepare recurring drafts without giving a remote service custody of your books.
  • Work offline, then export professional PDFs or a full .billingbird archive.
  • Choose from seven color themes with complete light and dark presentations.

Receipt capture designed for each device

On iPhone and iPad, scan receipts on-device and attach them to the right client or expense. On Mac, bring in receipt images and PDFs through Files-based import for the same organized recordkeeping workflow.

Continuity without a hosted bookkeeping account

No BillingBird account is required. Keep working records on the device in front of you, optionally move the active data set through your private iCloud account, and export a complete archive whenever you choose.

Accessible by design

BillingBird supports VoiceOver and Dynamic Type across supported Apple devices, with keyboard shortcuts on Mac and clear light and dark theme options. If you find an accessibility issue, please send us a note through the contact form.

Technical specifications

Platforms: iOS 17 or later, iPadOS 17 or later, and macOS 14 or later through Mac Catalyst.
Storage: local-first, with optional private iCloud backup and guarded device handoff.
