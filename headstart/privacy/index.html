HeadStart privacy

Your media server stays yours.

Effective November 2, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026

This policy covers HeadStart, provided by DeadStick Digital LLC. Website visits and contact-form submissions are also covered by the DeadStick Digital website privacy policy.

Data accessed locally

HeadStart runs locally on your server or NAS. It reads your local media library in read-only mode to prioritize and size SSD preload cache files. All preloaded cache data remains strictly on your local SSD storage.

Data we collect and how we use it

HeadStart has no user account, no analytics SDK, no telemetry, and no DeadStick-operated cloud service. We never receive your media library inventory, file names, watch history, or server configuration through HeadStart. If you email us or submit our website contact form, we receive only the message and contact details you voluntarily send so we can reply.

Your choices and deletion

Uninstalling HeadStart removes all local preload cache files and local metadata indexes from your SSD. To request deletion of any support correspondence you sent us, email contact@deadstick.net.
