Your menu bar, on your terms.

Open a tucked app.

Set it up once.

A welcome tour gets you started. Choose whether Dash opens at login, and revisit the tour from Settings whenever you need it.

Small app. Clear choices.

Dash lives in your menu bar. There is no account, subscription, trial, in-app purchase, or advertising.

Accessibility lets Dash identify menu-bar apps and activate and organize their items. Optional Screen Recording adds pixel previews; it is off by default. You can use native app icons and status text without enabling those previews.

Apple’s fixed menu-bar items, including the clock and Control Center, stay managed by macOS.
