How it works

Your menu bar, on your terms.

Command-drag to tuck.

Hold Command (⌘) and drag any movable menu-bar icon to the left of the DeadStick yoke to tuck it away, and keep everyday items to the right of the yoke.

Click or Option-click to reveal.

Click the yoke for the quick-reveal panel (“Show All in Menu Bar”), or Option-click (or right-click) the yoke to expand and tuck your menu bar in one click with optional 10s or 30s smart auto-hide.

Set it up once.

A two-step welcome tour gets you started. Choose whether Dash opens at login, and revisit the tour from Settings whenever you need it.

Architecture & Privacy

Small app. Clear choices.

Dash lives in your menu bar without adding Dock clutter. There is no account, subscription, trial, in-app purchase, or advertising.

Mac App Store edition: runs entirely inside the macOS App Sandbox with zero system permissions — no Accessibility or Screen Recording prompts, no cross-app inspection, and no network connections.

Direct-download builds: optionally use Accessibility to identify and activate individual menu-bar items in a dropdown, plus optional Screen Recording (off by default) for live pixel previews.

Apple’s fixed menu-bar items, including the clock and Control Center, stay managed by macOS.
