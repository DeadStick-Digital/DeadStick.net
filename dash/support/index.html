Getting started

  1. Open Dash and look for the yoke in the menu bar. Dash does not add a Dock icon.
  2. Follow the welcome tour. Grant Accessibility when you want Dash to identify, activate, and organize menu-bar items.
  3. Use Pin and Tuck to organize supported third-party items. Click the yoke to reach tucked apps.
  4. Open Settings to choose launch at login or to replay the welcome tour.

Common questions

Why does Dash ask for Accessibility?

It uses Accessibility to identify menu-bar apps, show names and status text, open individual tucked menus, and organize items. Manage this in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. After changing access, return to Dash Settings and use Re-check.

Do I need Screen Recording?

No. Optional live pixel previews are off by default. Native app icons and Accessibility status text work without Screen Recording. Enable it only if you want pixel previews; captures stay on your Mac. Manage this permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Why can’t I tuck the clock or Control Center?

macOS manages these fixed system items. Dash organizes supported third-party menu-bar items.

Where did a tucked item go?

Click the yoke to open the dropdown. Use the eye control to reveal the tucked area of the menu bar. Confirm that the other app is running and menu-bar tucking is enabled in Dash Settings.

What does the red dot mean?

A tucked app’s status badge has changed. Open the dropdown to clear the indicator.

How do I stop using Dash?

Reveal tucked items, turn off launch at login in Settings if enabled, and quit Dash. You can revoke Accessibility and Screen Recording access in System Settings.

Local data and privacy requests

There is no Dash account to delete. App preferences are stored on your Mac and may remain after uninstalling. Email us for help removing local settings, or to request deletion of support correspondence you previously sent. Please do not send passwords or private menu-bar contents.

You can withdraw optional access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security, turn off live previews in Dash Settings, or quit the app.
