DeadStick Dash privacy

Your menu bar stays yours.

Effective October 27, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026

This policy covers DeadStick Dash for macOS, provided by DeadStick Digital LLC, including current direct-download development builds and the planned Mac App Store edition. The Mac App Store edition is not yet available. We will update this policy before release if its data practices differ. Website visits and contact-form submissions are also covered by the DeadStick Digital website privacy policy.

Data accessed locally

Dash stores preferences and menu-bar organization on your Mac. With Accessibility permission, current development builds read supported menu-bar app names, icons, positions, and status text so you can identify, organize, and open those items. These builds use Accessibility and local input events for supported item actions.

Live pixel previews are off by default. If you turn them on and grant Screen Recording permission, Dash captures menu-bar item images for local display. Menu-bar inventory, status text, and captured images are not uploaded to DeadStick.

Data we collect and how we use it

Dash has no app account, advertising, analytics SDK, or DeadStick-operated cloud storage for app data. We do not receive your local settings, menu-bar inventory, status text, or preview images through the app. We do not sell this information or use it for tracking or targeted advertising.

If you email us or submit our website contact form, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. The contact form also processes connection and anti-spam information as described in the website policy. Do not include passwords, security codes, or unredacted private screenshots.

Distribution, updates, and third parties

Planned Mac App Store edition: Apple will handle app delivery and updates through the Mac App Store. Apple may process download, device, store-account, and diagnostic information under Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple settings. That edition will not use Sparkle or a separate DeadStick updater.

Direct-download development builds: these include the Sparkle update framework. When configured and used, update requests disclose connection information such as your IP address to the update feed or download host and may include app and system versions. Sparkle system-profile reporting is not enabled. Update delivery is still being prepared and is not promised in development builds.

Support and website providers: our email provider, Web3Forms contact-form provider, and website hosts process information needed to receive correspondence and serve the website. They do not receive your local menu-bar data through Dash. Service providers handling user data on our behalf must provide the same or equal protection described in this policy and required by applicable law. We do not share support data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics networks. Disclosures may be required by law or valid legal process.

Retention

Local settings remain on your Mac until removed; uninstalling the app may leave preferences behind. Menu-bar data and optional preview images are processed locally for the app’s display and are not stored on a DeadStick server. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to provide continuing support, resolve the request, or meet legal obligations. Where information must be retained by law, we limit its use to that purpose. Apple and website providers apply their own retention policies to information they process independently.

Your choices, consent, and deletion

  • Manage or revoke Accessibility and Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security. You can disable optional live pixel previews and launch at login in Dash Settings. Revoking access disables the features that depend on it; you can quit Dash at any time.
  • There is no Dash account and no server-side copy of local app settings for us to delete. For help removing preferences that remain on your Mac, email us; uninstalling alone may not remove them.
  • To request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, email contact@deadstick.net with the subject “DeadStick Dash privacy request.” Identify the email address used for the original correspondence. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on private records.
  • We will remove information we control when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. You do not need to install Dash to make a privacy request.
