DeadStick Dash privacy

Your menu bar stays yours.

Updated October 27, 2026

This policy covers DeadStick Dash for macOS, provided by DeadStick Digital LLC. For this website and contact form, see the DeadStick Digital privacy policy.

Local app data

Dash stores preferences and menu-bar organization on your Mac. It has no app account, advertising, analytics SDK, or DeadStick-operated cloud storage for your app data. We do not receive your menu-bar inventory, status text, or captured menu-bar images through Dash.

Accessibility

With your permission, Dash reads supported menu-bar app names, icons, positions, and status text, and uses Accessibility and local input events to activate and organize those items. This information is used locally to provide the dropdown and Pin / Tuck controls.

Optional pixel previews

Live pixel previews are off by default. If enabled with Screen Recording permission, Dash captures menu-bar item images for local display. Captures are not uploaded to DeadStick. You can turn previews off in Dash Settings and revoke access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Updates and network requests

The direct-download version includes the Sparkle update framework. When update checks are configured and used, requests to the update feed and download host necessarily disclose connection information such as your IP address and may include the app and system version. Sparkle system-profile reporting is not enabled. Update delivery is still being prepared; this page does not promise that automatic updates are available in development builds.

Support you choose to send

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to reply and troubleshoot. Our email and website form providers process correspondence as described in the website privacy policy. Avoid sending sensitive menu-bar content or unredacted screenshots. We retain correspondence as needed to provide support and meet legal obligations; you can contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Accessibility or Screen Recording in System Settings, disable live previews and launch at login in Dash Settings, and quit or uninstall Dash. Uninstalling the app may leave local preferences behind. Contact us for help removing those preferences. We do not have a server-side copy of your local Dash settings to delete.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@deadstick.net or use the contact form. Material changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
